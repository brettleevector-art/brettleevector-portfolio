04 / ABOUT

Analytical by training. Experimental by practice.

A background rooted in mathematics, diagnostic coaching, data engineering, and creative audio technology.

My background in mathematics shaped how I approach complexity: define the problem, look for patterns, test assumptions, and work through the details.

Alongside that analytical foundation, I work with Python and data tools, explore ML systems through hands-on experiments, build workflow automations, and bring an audio-production perspective to creative technology.

Whether evaluating ResNet18 checkpoints on mel-spectrogram audio, configuring n8n event pipelines with human-in-the-loop validation, or sculpting acoustic frequency responses, I focus on systems that are auditable, grounded, and built with taste.

I’m especially interested in the places where data systems, machine learning, and human creativity meet.
